HOW IT WORKS

The Omnitrix & the universe

⌚

The Omnitrix

A powerful device that stores alien DNA and lets its wearer transform into sampled species. Ben’s early use is imperfect: he often gets a different alien than expected, and the device has limits.

⌁

The Plumbers

A secret interplanetary peacekeeping organisation. Grandpa Max’s past as a Plumber gives the children access to knowledge, contacts and equipment far beyond an ordinary road trip.

◎

Alien biology

Each transformation has its own strengths, limitations and environmental needs. Choosing the right form matters as much as raw power.

FEATURE-LENGTH ADVENTURES

Classic-era movies

01

Secret of the Omnitrix

An animated feature in which the Omnitrix’s self-destruct sequence sends Ben, Gwen and Tetrax searching for Azmuth. It expands the device’s history and introduces important parts of its creator’s story.

Animated film • 2007
02

Race Against Time

A live-action adaptation set after the original series’ summer adventure, featuring Ben, Gwen, Grandpa Max and the threat of Eon. Its continuity differs from the main animated timeline.

Live action • 2007
03

Destroy All Aliens

An animated CGI movie returning to the original-series designs, with Ben’s alien transformations involved in a threat linked to the Omnitrix and the Plumbers.

CGI animated film • 2012
